About Us

Canrisk is an informational website aimed at providing the general public with accessible and reliable information about the most common types of cancer in the Salvadoran population: breast, cervical, prostate, stomach, and lung cancer.

Our project is named Canrisk because it combines two words: cancer and risk. We chose this fusion to give the platform an approachable and easy-to-remember identity.

This project was created by a team of students committed to providing reliable medical information to those who need it most.

What Drives Us

Our Mission

To provide clear and reliable medical information about breast, cervical, prostate, stomach, and lung cancer to the Salvadoran population, without unnecessary technical jargon.

Our Approach

We support both patients and their families by offering prevention tips, emotional support, and practical guidance all in one place.

Our Commitment

The privacy of our visitors is non-negotiable. Any information shared with us is handled with the utmost care and confidentiality.
