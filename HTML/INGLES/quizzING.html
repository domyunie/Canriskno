Awareness Quiz

How much do we really know about cancer? This quiz aims to evaluate the level of general information and understanding the community has about the disease, its definitions, and key preventive habits.


Your participation helps us identify critical areas where greater dissemination of educational content is required, understand people's relationship with medical check-ups, and strengthen collective empathy.

Concept and Real Knowledge

What is cancer to you? We analyze your self-perception of the disease, measuring whether you consider yourself poorly, moderately, or well informed regarding its implications.

Prevention and Medical Routine

Prevention saves lives. We record the frequency with which people undergo regular medical check-ups to evaluate their level of proactivity in health care.

Hospital Environment and Interest

We assess whether users know specialized medical centers for oncological treatments and measure their interest in continuing to stay informed to support their loved ones.

Empathy and Support

We seek to understand the human and social perspective of the process, analyzing individual willingness to accompany, visit, and understand the daily life of a patient undergoing treatment.

Why is it vital to stay informed?

Misinformation remains one of the main barriers to early detection and effective support.
