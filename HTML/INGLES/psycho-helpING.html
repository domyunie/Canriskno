Awareness Quiz


How much do we really know about cancer? This quiz aims to assess the general level of information and understanding the community has about the disease, its definitions, and key preventive habits.


Your participation helps us identify critical areas where more educational content is needed, understand people's relationship with medical check-ups, and strengthen collective empathy.

📝

Your answers are completely anonymous and only take a few minutes. Each piece of data collected helps us improve cancer education in the community.

Concept and Real Knowledge

What is cancer to you? We analyze your self-perception of the disease, measuring whether you consider yourself poorly, moderately, or well informed about its implications.

Prevention and Medical Routine

Prevention saves lives. We track how often people get regular medical check-ups to gauge their level of proactivity in caring for their health.

Hospital Environment and Interest

We assess whether users are aware of medical centers specialized in oncology treatment and measure their interest in continuing to learn in order to support their loved ones.

Empathy and Support

We seek to understand the human and social side of the process, analyzing people's willingness to accompany, visit, and understand the daily life of a patient in treatment.
